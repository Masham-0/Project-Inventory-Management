How Our Optimizer Works

Random Forest (RF)

We use a Random Forest Regressor to analyze historical sales data and accurately predict future demand over a 30-day horizon, capturing seasonal trends.

Particle Swarm Opt. (PSO)

Inspired by the choreography of a bird flock, PSO iteratively discovers the optimal inventory levels by navigating a cost function landscape to minimize expenses.

Economic Order Qty (EOQ)

EOQ calculates the perfect quantity to order. It balances the risk of accumulating excess stock (holding cost) versus the expense of too many orders (ordering cost).

Reorder Point (ROP)

The ROP tells you exactly when to reorder. It factors in your demand forecast and supplier lead time to prevent stockouts before they disrupt the business.
